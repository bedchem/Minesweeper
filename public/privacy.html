Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who we are

Minesweeper Multiplayer is a free, non-commercial hobby project run by its two developers, Ryhox and Nexor ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the processing of your personal data on this website within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003).

For anything about your data, including requests to exercise your rights, write to contact@pokyh.com.

2. What we process and why

Your account. When you register, we store your email address, a username you choose and a unique account ID. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google), which also stores your password in protected (hashed) form; we never see it. If you sign in with Google, GitHub, Discord or X, that provider sends us your account ID and, depending on the provider, your email address and display name. Legal basis: providing the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Your profile and game data. On our server we store your username, email address, the date your account was created, your previous usernames with the date of each change, your role (e.g. admin), the profile picture you upload, and the results of your games (mode, difficulty, number of players, date, won or lost, time and progress). We use this to show your profile, statistics and leaderboard rank. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

What is public. Your username, profile picture, statistics and leaderboard rank can be seen by anyone, including people who are not signed in. In a lobby, other players see your username, picture and ready status. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Lobby chat. Messages you send in a lobby chat are shown to the players in that lobby together with your username and picture. They are kept only in the server's memory (the last 50 messages per lobby) and are gone when the lobby closes; we do not save them to disk. If someone posts a link to an image, your browser loads it directly from that website, which then sees your IP address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Technical data. To run the site, connect you to games and protect it against abuse (for example limiting how many requests one IP address can make), our server processes your IP address and connection details. Rate-limit counters are kept in memory for at most 15 minutes. Admins can see the list of currently connected players (username, lobby, connection time) and a short log of recent actions such as "lobby created"; both exist only in memory and are lost when the server restarts. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Spam protection. The login and registration pages use Google reCAPTCHA, which processes your IP address and information about your browser and your interaction with the page to tell people and bots apart. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (protecting accounts from automated abuse).

3. Cookies and browser storage

  • Login session: Firebase keeps you signed in by storing a session token in your browser's storage. This is strictly necessary for the login to work.
  • Profile picture cache: we store your own account ID and picture version in your browser's local storage so your picture shows up immediately.
  • Cookie banner: the cookie cookieconsent_status remembers your choice in the banner for one year.

We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Who receives your data

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, reCAPTCHA, gstatic.com): sign-in, password reset emails, spam protection and the Firebase script files. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Sign-in providers (Google, GitHub, Discord, X): only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Content delivery networks: icons and some scripts are loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com (Cloudflare) and cdn.jsdelivr.net (jsDelivr). When your browser loads them, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information.

Your profile, statistics and pictures are stored on our own server. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else. Some of the services above may process data outside the European Union, in particular in the USA. Such transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 45 and 46 GDPR).

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account, profile, statistics and profile picture: until you delete your account. You can do that yourself at any time on the profile page; your login, profile data, game history and picture are then deleted immediately.
  • Lobby chat: until the lobby closes.
  • Connection data and rate-limit counters: at most 15 minutes, in memory only.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you (Art. 15);
  • have wrong data corrected (Art. 16); you can change your username yourself on the profile page;
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17);
  • restrict the processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21).

Just write to contact@pokyh.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

7. Children

In Italy you can create an account on your own from the age of 14 (Art. 2-quinquies of the Italian Personal Data Protection Code). If you are younger, a parent or guardian has to agree first.

8. Automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions about you and do not create profiles of you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

9. Changes

If the website changes the way it uses data, we will update this page. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
